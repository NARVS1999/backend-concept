Chapter 28 — The Attack

The climax — Bob Ewell attacks the children

Focus Walking home from the pageant, Scout and Jem are attacked. Jem's arm is broken. Bob Ewell is dead.

Key Details

  • Cecil Jacobs tries to scare them — anticlimactic
  • The walk home: dark, quiet
  • Footsteps follow — someone attacks
  • Jem is grabbed, arm broken, dragged
  • Scout in the ham costume is protected (wire mesh saves her)
  • A stranger pulls the attacker off Jem and kills him
  • Bob Ewell is dead — stabbed with his own knife
  • Heck Tate arrives, carries Jem home
