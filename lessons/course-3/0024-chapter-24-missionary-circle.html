Part Four: Aftermath and Resolution — Lesson 3 of 10

Chapter 24 — The Missionary Circle

Hypocrisy on display

Focus The Missionary Society meets at Finch house — the ladies discuss saving African natives while ignoring local injustice. Atticus brings news: Tom Robinson is dead.

Key Details

  • Ladies of the Missionary Circle discuss the "Mruna" tribe in Africa
  • Irony: obsessed with foreign missions, blind to local racism
  • Mrs. Merriweather complains about "darkies in this town"
  • Atticus interrupts: Tom Robinson is dead — shot 17 times trying to escape
  • "He took it as a matter of course" — Atticus's grief
  • Scout learns: "If your aunt could be a lady in these circumstances, I could too"

Study Task

Analysis Exercise

Contrast the missionary ladies' concern for the "Mruna tribe" with their prejudice against Maycomb's Black community. What is Lee satirizing?

Important Quote

"He took it as a matter of course" — Atticus on Tom's death

Atticus's quiet grief reveals the profound injustice — Tom's death was so predictable that even he accepted it as inevitable.

Summary
