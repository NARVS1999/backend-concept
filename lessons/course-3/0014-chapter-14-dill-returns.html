Chapter 14 — Dill Returns

Dill runs away from home

Focus Dill runs away from home and hides under Scout's bed. Tension between Atticus and Alexandra grows. Bob Ewell threatens Atticus.

Key Details

  • Jem breaks the childhood "code" by telling Atticus

  • Alexandra vs. Atticus: "You'd better take Calpurnia's side"

Study Task

Dill's vague description of his parents reveals emotional neglect — they don't actively hurt him, they simply don't care.
