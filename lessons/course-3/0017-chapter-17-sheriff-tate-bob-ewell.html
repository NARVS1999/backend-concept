Chapter 17 — Sheriff Tate and Bob Ewell

The prosecution's case begins

Focus Heck Tate testifies about Mayella's injuries — bruises on the right side of her face. Bob Ewell takes the stand, and Atticus reveals he is left-handed.

Key Details

  • Heck Tate: Mayella was beaten, bruises mostly on her right side
  • Bob Ewell testifies: vulgar, illiterate, aggressive
  • Atticus makes Bob sign his name — reveals he is left-handed
  • The Ewell property: trash-filled yard, poverty
  • The implication: a left-handed man (Bob) could have caused the right-sided injuries
  • Atticus's strategy: build reasonable doubt through physical evidence

Study Task

Analysis Exercise

Why is it significant that Mayella's bruises were on the right side of her face? How does Atticus use small physical details to dismantle Bob Ewell's testimony?
