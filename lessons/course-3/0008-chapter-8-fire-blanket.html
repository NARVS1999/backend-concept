Part One: Childhood in Maycomb — Lesson 8 of 8

Chapter 8 — Fire and the Blanket

Boo's unseen presence is revealed

Focus It snows in Maycomb — a rare event — and Miss Maudie's house burns down. In the chaos, Boo Radley places a blanket on Scout's shoulders without her noticing.

Key Details

  • First snow in Maycomb since 1885 — school canceled
  • Jem and Scout build a snowman (mud base, snow cover)
  • Miss Maudie's house catches fire overnight
  • Scout watches from the Radley gate, wrapped in a blanket
  • Atticus: "Boo Radley. You were so busy looking at the fire you didn't know it when he put the blanket around you"
  • Scout is stunned: "I damn near died"
  • Miss Maudie: not upset about losing her house — she'll have more yard for flowers

Study Task

Analysis Exercise

Scout didn't feel Boo approach. What does this say about his character — his gentleness, his stealth, his care? Miss Maudie responds to the fire with optimism — what does this reveal?

Important Quote

"Always wanted a smaller house, Jem. Gives me more yard" — Miss Maudie after the fire

Miss Maudie's optimism in the face of loss mirrors Atticus's definition of courage — grace under pressure.

Summary
