Part Three: The Trial — Lesson 4 of 10

Chapter 15 — The Lynch Mob

Scout's innocence disarms the mob

Focus A mob gathers to lynch Tom Robinson before the trial. Atticus waits at the jail to stop them. Scout's innocence disarms the mob.

Key Details

  • Heck Tate and others warn Atticus about potential trouble
  • Atticus sits outside the jail, reading, guarding Tom
  • The Old Sarum bunch (Cunninghams) arrive to lynch Tom
  • The children follow Atticus and find him at the jail
  • Scout recognizes Mr. Cunningham — starts a conversation about entailment
  • Mr. Cunningham is shamed: "I'll tell him you said hey, little lady"
  • The mob disperses — Scout unknowingly saves Tom

Study Task

Analysis Exercise

Scout's simple conversation disarms the mob. How does this scene demonstrate the power of Atticus's "climb into his skin" lesson?

Important Quote

"I'll tell him you said hey, little lady" — Mr. Cunningham

Scout's recognition of Mr. Cunningham as an individual breaks the mob's anonymity and shames them into leaving.
