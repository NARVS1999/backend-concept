Part Four: Aftermath and Resolution — Lesson 1 of 10

Chapter 22 — The Aftermath

Coping with injustice

Focus The town reacts to the verdict. Miss Maudie consoles Jem, and Bob Ewell threatens Atticus.

Key Details

  • Jem cries — his faith in the world is broken
  • Miss Maudie consoles him: "There are some men in this world who were born to do our unpleasant jobs for us. Your father is one of them"
  • Atticus is "the only man who can keep a jury out so long on a case like that"
  • Bob Ewell threatens Atticus — spits in his face at the post office
  • Atticus wipes his face and walks away — quiet dignity

Study Task

Analysis Exercise

Miss Maudie says some men are "born to do our unpleasant jobs." What does this mean about Atticus's role in Maycomb?

Important Quote

"There are some men in this world who were born to do our unpleasant jobs for us. Your father is one of them." — Miss Maudie

Miss Maudie articulates Atticus's role — he does the hard, necessary work that others are too afraid or prejudiced to do.
