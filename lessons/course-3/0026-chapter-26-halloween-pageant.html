Chapter 26 — The Halloween Pageant

Hypocrisy, and preparation for the climax

Focus School resumes, and Scout prepares for the Halloween pageant. She notices hypocrisy: Miss Gates hates Hitler but is racist at home.

Key Details

  • Scout is in third grade now
  • School Halloween pageant: Scout is a ham (her costume)
  • Miss Gates teaches about democracy vs. Hitler's persecution of Jews
  • "We don't believe in persecuting anyone" — Miss Gates
  • Scout overheard Miss Gates at the courthouse: "It's time somebody taught them a lesson"
  • Scout: "How can she hate Hitler so bad and then be ugly to folks right at home?"
  • Jem is furious: "Don't you ever mention that again!"

Analysis Exercise
