Part One: Childhood in Maycomb — Lesson 6 of 8

Chapter 6 — Jem Loses His Pants

The night visit to the Radley house

Focus On Dill's last night in Maycomb, the children sneak to the Radley house to peek through a window. They're spotted, Jem loses his pants, and returns later to retrieve them — finding them mysteriously mended.

Key Details

  • Dill's last night before returning to Meridian
  • The children creep to the Radley house, look through a loose shutter
  • A shadow of a man with a hat appears — they run
  • Jem's pants catch on the fence — he abandons them
  • Nathan Radley fires a shotgun — neighbors gather
  • Jem goes back at 2 AM to retrieve his pants
  • Jem returns "moody and silent" — won't tell Scout what happened

Study Task

Analysis Exercise

Jem's pants were "sewed up" and "folded across the fence" when he retrieved them. What does this tell us about Boo? Why does Jem keep this secret?

Important Quote

"Jem's white shirt-tail dipped and bobbed like a small ghost" — Scout watching Jem run

The image of Jem running creates suspense and foreshadows the supernatural feeling of the Radley mystery.

Quiz

Summary

  • The children's dare goes wrong — they are discovered at the Radley house
  • Boo secretly mends Jem's pants — the first act of unseen kindness
  • Jem's silence shows he is beginning to see Boo as a real person
