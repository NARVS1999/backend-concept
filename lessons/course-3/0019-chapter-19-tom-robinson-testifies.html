Part Three: The Trial — Lesson 8 of 10

Chapter 19 — Tom Robinson Testifies

Tom tells his side of the story

Focus Tom takes the stand and tells his side: Mayella grabbed him, kissed him, and Bob Ewell caught them. Tom ran because he was afraid.

Key Details

  • Tom testifies clearly, respectfully
  • Mayella asked him to do chores regularly — always paid, no trouble
  • "She reached up and kissed me right on the mouth"
  • Bob Ewell appeared at the window — screamed at Mayella
  • Tom ran because of fear: "I was bound to go to jail"
  • Tom's left arm: crippled from a cotton gin accident
  • Link Deas: "I've never had a speck of trouble out of him"

Study Task

Analysis Exercise

Tom is physically incapable of committing the crime, and the evidence supports his story. Why is the jury still likely to convict?

Important Quote

"I was scared, suh... if I was to get in a fight I'd lose" — Tom Robinson

Tom explains why he ran — not guilt, but fear of being falsely accused by a white woman.

Summary

  • Tom gives a clear, consistent testimony
  • The truth emerges: Mayella made advances, Bob Ewell caught them
  • Tom's physical disability proves he could not have committed the crime
