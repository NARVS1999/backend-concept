The Average Genius

Not everyone is a genius — but that doesn't mean you can't be great. Zanka, once the top student at the Hell Guard Academy, realized his limits when Hyo surpassed him effortlessly. He discovered that true strength doesn't come from innate talent, but from dedication and persistence. He accepted being "average," but that never stopped him from striving to improve.

In backend development, you'll encounter people who seem like naturals — they learn fast, solve problems instantly. But Zanka, with his Lovely Assistaff and his vibration sensing ability, proved that intelligence isn't just about speed — it's about observation, about understanding every move before you make it. In this course, you'll learn to be resilient like Zanka — facing challenges head-on instead of running from them.

Zanka's greatest lesson: never underestimate the mind of an "average" person. An ordinary person, when armed with determination and strategy, can overcome any genius. In backend development, it's the same — you don't have to be special to be exceptional. You just need the right training and the right mindset.
